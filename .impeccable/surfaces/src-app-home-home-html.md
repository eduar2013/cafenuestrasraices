# Inicio (/)

Scope: home page plus the shared header (contact bar, side nav) and footer, which also appear on /tienda. Mode: Persuade. Inherits the shop's world (DESIGN.md): no new identity.

Audience: locals deciding whether to come and what they can taste; online buyers who land here first. Job: understand in seconds what the place is (a tasting bar for many Colombian coffees and methods), how to visit, and how to buy.

Proof: real shop photo, history video, live catalog (origins map, products), real address and hours. Methods offered (user-confirmed): espresso and milk drinks; V60 and Chemex; French press; AeroPress, siphon and moka. History copy is provisional (user). Carousel stays; the user will replace its photos and copy with real material later.

Anti-goals: invented claims (roasting, fair trade, traceability, awards, reviews); kicker/eyebrow labels above headings; decorative folklore.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page explains the visit as four painted steps (Llega, Prueba, Elige, Llévalo), each one a real section of the page. Refuses the stack of equal centred sections the old home was and the generic hero-plus-cards café page.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited from DESIGN.md (sober painted wall): lime-wash ground, ink lettering and filete, one deep green for actions, stone for chosen state, Shrikhand only for signs, names, prices and step numbers; Albert Sans for everything else. Painted step numbers are the one new device and they carry real wayfinding.

STORY: The visitor reads what the place is, sees how a visit works, finds the address and whether it is open now, sees what methods they can taste, explores where the coffees come from, and can buy them online or pick them up. The history and the carousel follow the four steps, so the numbered path is never interrupted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: painted sign "Tu visita", h1 "Prueba cafés de toda Colombia en Sabaneta", one lead line, then four numbered steps as links (Llega · address and hours, Prueba · methods, Elige · origins, Llévalo · shop), the step list being the page's own navigation. Right column: the shop photo inside an ink filete. Primary actions: "Cómo llegar" and "Ver la tienda".
